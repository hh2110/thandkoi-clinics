Every figure comes from `stats`, an apps.pipeline.dashboard.DashboardStats built live in ClinicDashboardPage.get_context from DailyAggregate rows — never from a stored field on this page, which has none (D1). No AI-written prose anywhere on this page (D12). No JavaScript for anything load-bearing, by construction rather than as a fallback (D9): the presets are plain links carrying the range in the query string, and the From/To form is a GET form with a visible Apply submit. Nothing here auto-submits, so nothing here needs a script. The chart is server-rendered SVG for the same reason (D2) — see apps.pipeline.footfall_chart, whose geometry this shares with the reports index, styled through its own `dash-chart` block rather than that page's `ri-funding-mix` one. 2026-07-25: the chart's per-bar hover tooltip is the one exception, and it is purely additive. The `data-funding-mix`/`data-funding-mix-hit` attribute contract is the reports index's, wired up by the sitewide static/js/funding-mix-chart.js; the reader was already missing the hover figures they get on that page. With the script blocked or broken the chart, its axis labels and the "View as table" fallback below it are all unchanged — nothing on this page reads from the hit rects. `pct` deliberately means two things, following DashboardStats' docstring: share of total_visits in funding_rows/age_bands, but share of the larger of Female/Male in gender_rows — which is why the Gender card renders no "%" text at all, only bars scaled to each other. Zakat% + Regular% can sum to under 100 when a range holds unknown-payment visits; that shortfall is left implicit and the counts beside every bar stay authoritative (D5). Revenue surfaces (the 4th KPI card and the "Revenue by service" table) are written against `has_revenue` and are inert today — `dashboard.has_revenue` returns False until the clinic software ships fee columns (D6/D13), so Phase 2 lights them up with no template work. As of the 2026-07-25 handoff revision (Plan 18) that is the whole of the gate: the split section's layout no longer changes with it, because the table now sits full width above the two side cards rather than beside them.
